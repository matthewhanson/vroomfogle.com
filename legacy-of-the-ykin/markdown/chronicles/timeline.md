# Overview & Timeline

**Chapter 1 — The Borley Boys.** Newly graduated from Borley, Kawill, Bloth’roc, Zalcoat, and Beltharr are delivered to the Temple of Chakor, where Ba’bak’s Essænce trials and Zalcoat’s brutal execution teach them what obedience costs. Under Kulkan’s direction they learn The Plan, take up Adzel’s Skull for infiltration, and head north with Armand—surviving ruined villages, assassins, and an Essænce storm as a mysterious key and Locust intrigue pull them toward Achren.

**Chapter 2 — The City of Achren.** The road ends at Achren in the wake of the Priest-Lords’ departure: the city still bustles, but authority has splintered and every faction bargains for leverage. At Tameki House, Denivor and allies split off to hunt the Implementor while Kulkan’s contingent navigates contacts, shifting loyalties, and escalating violence inside the city; betrayal turns lethal, a demon is loosed, and the survivors begin following Zeki Balaban and the Bridge of Vows toward deeper mysteries.

**Chapter 3 — Into the Portal.** The Bridge of Vows becomes a threshold: a sustained flute-song holds open a black portal, and the party steps into an ancient Earthwarden chamber packed with mechanisms, crystals, and recordings from a dead age. With a newly revealed key and allies converging on both sides of the frame, the mission shifts from local intrigue to navigating stranger roads—where portals, artifacts, and old powers start reshaping what the Plan and the Implementor truly mean.

*Note: All dates are in the **Third Era of Ire**.*

## Pre-6049

| Date | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| c. 500 | (Tanara) Ochu people feeling oppression in northern Emer crossed the Melurian Straits and came to the southern shores of Tanara. |
| **c. 500 - 2000** | (**Tanara**) The **Y’kin** (**Ochu**) settle in the hills of the **Grey Mountains**, building cities and structures into the cliffs and develop a rich culture dedicated to art and science. |
| **c. 2000 - 3000** | (**Tanara**) **Cloudlords** emerge in **Tanara**, astride the majestic **Streardon** and there is a period of peace in most of **Tanara**. These shifts in the geopolitical landscape change the **Y’kin** ruling class where followers of **Yugal**, *Father of Gods*, take power. |
| **c. 3000 - 5000** | (**Tanara**) The **Priest-Lords** of **Yugal** rule for thousands of years. The life of a typical **Y’kin** is brutish and short, but surprisingly they are mostly happy. Their culture develops around the idea of rebirth and procreation. The **Y’kin** population grows, but they remain constrained in a small area of **Tanara**. There are occasional forays into **Sulini** or **Myri** lands, but not enough to keep pace with their growth. |
| **c.5000**  | (**Tanara**) Tensions come to a head between the **Y’kin** and the **Myri**. The **Duranaki**, **Sulini**, and **Myri** join together and push the **Y’kin** back into the hills. |
| **c.5000 - 6000** | (**Tanara**) The **Y’kin** quietly rebuild, but live in cramped spaces where disease is rampant. The **Priest-Lords** rule becomes more violent and dark. Executions are common. |
| **c. 6000** | (**Tanara**) The **Y’kin** devise **The Plan** (of **Yugal**, their God) which is, simply put, the extermination of the other realms in **Tanara**. Recognizing the need for specialized forces to execute their ambitions, the **Priest-Lords** found the **Ixtal Institutes** in a secluded region of southern **Tanara**.  |
| **6039 - 40** | (**Tanara**) *The Ice-Winter*. An unusually harsh winter with heavy snows continuing into spring causes hardships throughout **Tanara**. Snow-gark attacks on the **Duranaki** become intolerable. The warrior **T’Kaal Arain** gathers a force and mounts a series of counterattacks, driving the Garks northward. The **Y’kin** fare better, going on a massive extermination campaign such that Garks attacks are unheard of for several years.![][image3] |
| **6043** | (**Rhakhaan**) **Italana** dies and her son **Jerrin III** takes the throne of **Rhakhaan**, but he refuses the **Phoenix Crown**. His younger cousin, **Frelik**, steals the crown and retreats north, claiming to be the true King. (**Tanara**) Gark raiders, driven from **Y’kin** lands attack the **Myri** in the foothills. |
| **6045** | (**Tanara**) **T’Kaal Arain** becomes First Speaker for the **Duranaki**. |
| **6046** | (U-Lyshak) Prince Halek of Helyssa is slain while on a hunting trip; the realm teeters on the verge of anarchy. Prince Halek’s son Kier vanishes that same evening and a search is begun. The Priest of Yaarth accepts the Regency and maintains order. |
| **6045 - 48** | (Rhakhaan) Frelik “The Pretender'', aided by mercenaries from Saralis, assaults the northern borders of Rhakhaan. He makes slow progress southwards. |
| **6047** | (Tanara) The Y’kin start making final preparations for going to war with the Myri as part of The Plan. |
