# The Cast

## Characters

### **Amaraq**

Marked by loss and inked with a wolf’s sigil, Amaraq learned early that survival favors the unseen. A former smuggler turned Vow Crafter for the Locusts, he navigates Achren’s new order with a steady hand, never forgetting how quickly power can turn predatory.

### **Armand**

Once a mere servant in **Chakor** after being abandoned by his mother, **Armand** rose through the ranks due to his unmatched craftsmanship and insatiable thirst for knowledge. Fluent in multiple languages, he frequently journeyed within the **Y'kin** borders, engaging with diverse cultures and seeking out teachers to expand his horizons. Although he respects the **Priests** and their reverence for **Yugal**, *Father of Gods*, **Armand**'s understanding is deepened by his knowledge of the broader pantheon of Charon's gods, and he questions the teachings of **Chakor**, especially as tensions with the **Myri** escalate towards war.

### **Bar’cal**

An older graduate from **Borley**, **Bar’cal** was accompanying **Kulkan** until he exposed himself as a traitor and aided with the killing of **Kulkan** in **Achren**.

### **Beltharr**

A fervent assassin from the **Borley Monastery**, **Beltharr** embodies the principle of trusting no one, having killed his own twin, **Baltharr**, on their graduation day from **Borley**. He harbors a secret longing for the freedom of the seas, often reminiscing about stories from his childhood and illegally praying to **Shaal**, *the Destroyer - Lord of the Seas*. While committed to the **Y'kin**'s ambitions of empire, he maintains a wary relationship with his companions.

### **Bembe Tun**

One of the Tun brothers, Bembe is a master of arms.

### **Bloth’roc**

An outsider among the **Yinka** due to his disability, **Bloth’roc** prioritizes his own well-being, adeptly navigating **Yinka** culture without embracing their ideologies. His impairments, particularly in hearing and speech, have led many to underestimate him, viewing him as unintelligent; however, this often works to his advantage, allowing him to maintain an air of mystery. While others like **Kawill** see him as a brother, **Bloth'roc** remains wary of those around him, especially the perceptive Priest **Kulkan**, and is focused on surviving, regardless of the outcome of **The Plan**.

### **Denivor**

A rare **Sulini** merchant who passes through towns with his silent **Changramai** bodyguards. He entertains locals with ancient tales, which are not favored by the priesthood.

### **Kawill**

Originally a devout believer from the village of **Clent**, **Kawill** grew disillusioned with temple teachings, developing an intense focus on combat training due to his inherent aptitude for fighting. Recognized by his instructors for his prowess, he saw the Faith mainly as an avenue for his violent tendencies. Despite his limited intellect, **Kawill**'s reputation as a formidable assassin earned him respect and trust from figures like **Ba’bak** and **Xoc** the Prior, who viewed him as dedicated to **Yugal** and critical to **The Plan**. However, encountering different races like **Denivor** and confrontations such as with **Zuma** have begun to shake **Kawill**'s previously simplistic worldview, making him question the motives and teachings he once accepted without doubt.

### **Nawiss**

An enigmatic old man, who responds to "**Nawiss**", or even "Old man". He is ancient in appearance, looking more like some little monster than a **Yinka**. His speech is thick with accent and frequently mumbles, it is not clear if he is talking to himself or someone in particular at these times.He’s either confused or just crazy. He asks questions about **Yinka** and other races in **Tanara**, of the **Priesthood** and of the war. He usually dismisses most questions to him as unimportant, or he says he does not know. He claims he has been asleep and there is much he has forgotten, but he has awoken due to the appearance of **The Implementor**, a being of chaos and destruction.

### **Zeki Balaban**

An old wise scholar from **Achren**, **Zeki** is an expert in ancient artifacts, especially those of **Tethior** and **Krelij**.

### **Zuma**

One of two **Changramai** monks that are employed by **Denivor**. **Zuma** is middle-aged for a **Yinka**. Of average height and build he does not stand out as physically superior but he is clearly an accomplished and skilled fighter.

## Deceased

### **Kulkan**

A distinguished Priest of **Chakor**, **Kulkan** is known for his proficiency in *Mentalism*. Initially a student at **Borley**, his exceptional talents led him to **Chakor** for advanced training in *Mentalism*, and he has since risen to the position of Coadjutor of **Ixtal**, working directly under the authority of the influential Priest-Lord **Ba'bak**. **Kulkan** holds a commanding role within his group and, while his true intentions remain a mystery, he appears deeply committed to their cause. His close association with the powerful **Ba'bak** suggests he is privy to significant information and strategies related to the **Y'kin**'s overarching goals. **Kulkan** was killed in **Achren** by **Beltharr**.

### **Zalcoat**

**Zalcoat**, an assassin graduate from **Borley** monastery, hailed from **Clent** where boys were chosen by **Priest-Lords** for training. At **Borley**, under the guidance of **S'rn**, he secretly learned about the broader world beyond the **Yinka**-centric teachings. However, **Zalcoat**'s life ended tragically when he was accused by **Kulkan** of conspiracy and subsequently killed by **Ba'bak**.

## NPCs

### **Akna**

A fierce Yinka warrior with an insatiable bloodlust, Akna harbors deep-seated disdain for the dominant Priests. As Hadwin's sister, she stands as a defiant member of the Locusts, always ready to challenge the prevailing powers.

### **Ba'bak**

The fearsome Priest-Lord of Ixtal, Ba’bak casts a dark shadow from the Temple of Chakor. His physique is a testament to raw power, every sinewy muscle hinting at the brute force he wields, evidenced by the chilling manner in which he dispatched Zalcoat with brass knuckles. Yet, his true menace lies in his mastery of the Mentalism, which he employs as deftly as he does physical violence. Under the guise of testing faith to The Plan, he subjects the Y’kin to unspeakable torments, his tyranny unmatched and unchallenged.

### **Dirtka**

Reputedly the blacksmith's daughter from Trulka, Dirtka is an enigma wrapped in a puzzle. While her ties to the Locusts are undeniable, she remains a step removed from their core, her loyalties ever-shifting. Her passionate dalliance with Armand is as tempestuous as her history with him, having once teetered on the edge of ending his life. Yet, in a twist of fate, she now emerges as a potential ally, her motives as mysterious as her origins.

### **Hadwin, The Quartermaster**

While masterfully overseeing the Yinkan army's logistics as their trusted quartermaster, Hadwin covertly aligns with the Locusts, a faction opposing the war. His dual allegiance showcases a complex character, leveraging his position to clandestinely work against the very conflict he outwardly supports.

### **The Tun Brothers**

Identical triplets possessing an uncanny mental connection, the Tuns can communicate with each other over vast distances without uttering a word. They claim to be members of the military, although they seem to act independently from authority, only sharing their actual goals with each other. Akto, with an affinity for nature, thrives in the wild, understanding terrains and tracking like no other. Bembe, the weapons-master, can wield, craft, and understand any weapon with unparalleled expertise. Lastly, Coba, the scholar among them, delves deep into ancient scripts, providing intelligence and deciphering secrets critical to their cause.
